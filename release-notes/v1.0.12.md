# HW Blackbox 1.0.12

A laptop's battery in Diagnose. The settings and the history carry over.

### New: the battery in Diagnose

On a laptop, the Diagnose tab now has a **Battery** check:

- **Its wear:** how much of the charge it held new it still holds ("Holds 30 Wh of the 55 Wh it was made for: 45 %
  worn"). Under 20 % worn is fine; 20 to 40 % is normal for a battery a few years old, with how to slow it (a charge
  limit, less heat); 40 % or more says to plan a replacement, and to keep it plugged in for long work meanwhile, since
  a worn battery can cut out near empty before Windows warns.
- **How it was used over the last day:** the time on battery, how fast it drained, and how long a full charge would
  last at that rate.
- **How low it ran:** down to 5 % or less on battery, it says so, and how to make Windows hibernate at its critical
  level so running out never loses work.
- For the battery's whole history, it points to Windows' own `powercfg /batteryreport`.

Desktops have no battery, and no Battery check.

### Install or update

From 1.0.10 or later: the page offers this version; click **Update**. From 1.0.9 or earlier: download
**HWBlackbox-win64.zip** below, unzip it, and double-click **`Install.cmd`**. An update keeps the settings and the
history. New here? See [Install](https://github.com/jamesccupps/HW-Blackbox#install): it offers the PawnIO driver if
it's missing.
