# HW Blackbox 1.0.9

Laptops, why a PC went off, and the installer offers the sensor driver. The settings and the history carry over.

### The installer offers PawnIO

The CPU's and the motherboard's sensors need the PawnIO driver. Until now the installer only warned when it was
missing, and the warning was easy to miss. Now it asks: **Install PawnIO now? [Y/n]**. Y (or Enter) installs it with
winget, in the installer's window, and HW Blackbox starts with the sensors working. N goes on without it. Without
winget, it opens PawnIO's download page. With nobody to answer (a scripted install), it leaves it out, as before;
`install.ps1 -PawnIO yes` installs it without asking.

Already installed without it? Run `Install.cmd` again and answer yes: the settings and history are kept.

### Laptops

On a laptop, 1.0.8 reported two problems that weren't: "No rail voltages" (a laptop has no power supply rails to
read) and "No CPU temperature (the sensor driver may be gone)" when the driver had never been installed.

- **The PawnIO driver** is now checked directly. When it isn't installed or isn't running, that's the one problem
  shown, saying what it costs (the CPU's temperature and power; on a desktop, the rails too) and how to install it
  (run `Install.cmd` again). Diagnose gives the steps.
- **No rails is no problem where there are none:** on a laptop, or a desktop board whose sensor chip
  LibreHardwareMonitor can't read. The page says so in a note instead. A board chip that answered before and doesn't
  now is still a problem.
- **The battery:** on a laptop the Overview's Board card becomes a Battery card: the charge, plugged in or on
  battery, the rate it charges or drains, and its wear. The 5-second log records the charge and whether it was
  plugged in, and Charts can draw the charge.
- **Without the driver**, the CPU's power and an Intel GPU's power are no longer shown as a made-up 0 W.
- An Intel GPU's memory is labelled **shared**: it borrows the PC's own.
- The GPU card names an Intel GPU, rather than "GPU".

### Why it went off

Every power-off report now says what Windows recorded about it (its Kernel-Power 41 record): **a blue screen** (with
the stop code), **the power button held down**, **going into or coming out of sleep**, or nothing (the power cut, or
a freeze too hard to record anything). On a laptop it also says what the battery was at just before. Reports written
before 1.0.9 get the same answer, looked up from Windows' records while they still have it.

Diagnose's advice follows from that: a blue screen, a forced power-off, a sleep problem or a flat battery each get
their own steps, and a laptop no longer gets advice about power supplies and wall sockets.

### Fixes

- **Intel CPU temperature:** the rails log (4 a second) only knew AMD's sensor names, so on an Intel CPU its CPU
  columns stayed blank. The 5-second log took the highest of the CPU's temperatures, and on Intel those include each
  core's "distance to TjMax" (how far below its limit it runs, higher when cooler), so an idle Intel CPU could be
  logged hotter than it was. Both now read the package temperature.
- The Overview's power-off card says when the last one was even when there are no readings from before it (one that
  came before HW Blackbox was installed): "before" the time the PC came back on.
- A log file begun by an older version gets the new column names written again, partway down, at the update, and the
  page reads the rows after them by those.

### Install or update

Download **HWBlackbox-win64.zip** below, unzip it, and double-click **`Install.cmd`**. An update keeps the settings
and the history. New here? See [Install](https://github.com/jamesccupps/HW-Blackbox#install): it offers the PawnIO driver
if it's missing.
